About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Chemistry at GITAM University, Visakhapatnam, India. My research is rooted in computational and theoretical chemistry, with a focus on understanding complex molecular phenomena in soft matter, biological systems, and novel solvents.

Before joining GITAM, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Toronto (2016–2023) under Prof. Hue Sun Chan, Canada Research Chair, where I developed coarse-grained and analytical models for liquid-liquid phase separation in membraneless organelles — one of the most exciting frontiers in modern biophysics.

I completed my Ph.D. under Prof. Ranjit Biswas at S. N. Bose National Centre for Basic Sciences (Jadavpur University) in 2016, investigating heterogeneity and chemical dynamics in molten multi-component systems, including deep eutectic solvents and ionic liquids.

For my M.Sc. project at IIT Guwahati, I worked under Prof. Sandip Paul on hydrogen bond properties at the liquid-vapour interface of water-DMSO mixtures.

I am a recipient of the prestigious Ramanujan Fellowship (SERB, 2023) and am passionate about mentoring graduate and undergraduate students in computational research.
