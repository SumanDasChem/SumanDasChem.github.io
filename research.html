Research

Research Interests

Current Focus Areas

🧬

Liquid-Liquid Phase Separation in Membraneless Organelles

Developing sequence-dependent coarse-grained and analytical models to understand how intrinsically disordered proteins undergo phase separation — with direct relevance to gene regulation, stress response, and neurodegenerative disease.

💧

Deep Eutectic Solvents & Ionic Liquids

Using all-atom molecular dynamics simulations to probe microstructure, hydrogen-bond dynamics, dielectric relaxation, ion clustering, and transport properties in DESs and ILs — green alternatives to conventional solvents.

⚛️

Intrinsically Disordered Proteins

Investigating hydration dynamics, conformational behavior, and binding mechanisms of IDPs using theory and simulation — exploring how amino acid sequence encodes biological function without a fixed 3D structure.

🤖

Machine Learning Interatomic Potentials

Developing and benchmarking ML-based interatomic potentials (DeePMD, MACE) trained on ab initio MD data for electrolyte systems — bridging quantum accuracy with classical simulation speed.

🖥️

Quantum Computing in Chemistry

Exploring applications of quantum algorithms to chemical simulation problems — an emerging research interest at the boundary of chemistry, physics, and computer science.

🔬

Protein Stability in Non-Aqueous Solvents

Understanding how non-aqueous environments — including DES and ionic liquids — alter protein structure, stability, and function, with implications for green chemistry and industrial biotechnology.
