About Me

Background & Biography

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Chemistry at GITAM University, Visakhapatnam, India.

Before joining GITAM, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Toronto (2016–2023) under Prof. Hue Sun Chan working on Liquid-liquid phase separation of Intrinsically Disordered Proteins (IDPs).

I completed my Ph.D. under Prof. Ranjit Biswas at S. N. Bose National Centre for Basic Sciences (Jadavpur University) in 2016.

I am a recipient of the prestigious Ramanujan Fellowship (SERB, 2023)
